What you get

A whole TV box, without the box.

TeleTV runs Google's own Android TV system in a normal window you can resize, snap or send full screen.

Real Android TV

Google TV with the Play Store, or plain Android TV if you'd rather. The same home screen, settings and apps as a TV box.

Import APKs

Drag APK files onto the window or browse for them. They install straight into Android and open. Bundles (.apks, .xapk) work too.

Built-in Downloader

Type a web address or a direct APK link. When an app finishes downloading, press Install. Favourites remember your usual sites.

Keyboard, mouse or remote

Arrow keys are the D-pad, Esc is Back, Home is Home. Click and scroll inside apps. Bluetooth TV remotes just work.

Opens in seconds

Quick start saves where you were when TeleTV closes, so next time Android picks up instantly.

Updates itself

New versions of TeleTV install from inside the app, and Android updates come straight from Google in the background.

Setup

Install, open, done.

Run TeleTV-Setup.exe

A normal Windows installer. No admin rights needed.

Let it fetch Android

The first time, TeleTV downloads Android TV straight from Google (about 2 GB) and asks you to accept Google's licence.

Start watching

If Windows Hypervisor Platform is off, TeleTV switches it on and asks for one restart. After that it's straight in every time.

Questions

Good to know.

Where does Android come from?

Straight from Google's servers, the same Android emulator and TV system images Android developers use. TeleTV downloads them on first launch under Google's Android SDK licence, which you accept during setup. Nothing is modified.

Do Netflix, Prime Video and Disney+ work?

No. Those apps require Google's certified-device checks for their video protection, and no Android emulator passes them. Most other video apps, media players and IPTV apps work.

Why won't an app install?

The most common reason is that the app is built only for ARM phone processors. TeleTV explains the reason when an install fails, in plain English.

Does it work alongside Hyper-V, WSL or VirtualBox?

Yes. TeleTV uses Windows Hypervisor Platform, which works alongside Hyper-V, WSL 2 and current versions of VirtualBox.

Where are my files kept?

Everything lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\TeleTV. Uninstalling asks whether to remove it, so you can keep Android and your apps if you plan to reinstall.
